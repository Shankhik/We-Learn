Mouse Events:
mouseOver:
1. Bubbles up the DOM hierarchy.
2. Triggered when the pointer enters the element or its child elements.
3. Useful when you want to detect hover on an element and any of its descendants.

mouseEnter:
1. Does not bubble.
2. Triggered only when the pointer enters the element itself (not its children).
3. More specific and avoids unnecessary event handling for child elements.
